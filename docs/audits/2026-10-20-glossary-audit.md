# Glossary audit — 2026-10-20

Live table had **367 terms**. This pass deletes **87**, leaving **280**. Every deletion and every tag/bearing below is listed so it can be checked line by line. Items marked **(low confidence)** are the ones I would most like a second opinion on.

## Rules applied

1. **Delete** when the definition only restates the words, when the entry is a unit everyone knows, when it duplicates a kept entry, or when it is a *substance* (vitamins, minerals) that belongs in the catalog. A glossary entry for "Magnesium" would turn every mention of magnesium in a post into a tooltip and compete with the substance link.
2. **Keep short** Latin routes (Intravenous, Subcutaneous, …) and the units that are genuinely confused (mcg vs mg, IU, cc).
3. **Tags** say how a word is used: `slang`, `safety`, `lab-report`. The old *Slang* category is dissolved; its seven terms move to a real category and carry the `slang` tag, so Cycle can be Usage Patterns *and* slang.
4. **Bearings** (0–5, from the site's 20) only where the term meaningfully relates to a goal. Research-method words get none; a term about reading studies has no goal.
5. **Detail** (the longer paragraph) is left empty in this pass. Writing 300 paragraphs blind would be the half-assed version; they should be added where a term actually trips people up, starting from search logs and support questions.
