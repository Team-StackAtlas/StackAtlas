## Why StackAtlas exists

Most of us ended up here the same way. You had a question about something
you were taking, or thinking about taking, and the places you looked gave
you nothing useful. A forum thread where the top answer was "it's good." A
review that read like it was written by the brand. Five people telling you
five different times to take it, five different doses, and nobody agreeing
on whether it did anything at all. And somewhere in the back of your mind,
the question of whether the person you were reading was even a person.

StackAtlas is an attempt to do better than that, together.

## What we are

A community of people sharing what they actually did and what actually
happened, in enough detail to be useful to the next person. We encourage you
to share as much of your health journey as you are comfortable sharing:
the dose, the timing, the brand, what changed, what did not, and what you
would do differently. The specifics are the point. "It worked for me" helps
no one. "Three weeks at 400 mg with dinner, sleep improved, stomach did
not" helps everyone.

Alongside the community sits the evidence. Every substance in the Atlas
carries the studies behind it, linked to the source, verified against the
original record, and labeled honestly for how strong they are. We are big
on high-quality sources, and we would rather show you a single good trial
than a page of citations nobody checked.

## What we believe

Mainstream consensus is usually right, and it is where you should start.
It is also sometimes behind, not because anyone is hiding anything, but
because research takes money and time, and a lot of what people in this
space care about has not had either yet. We hold both of those ideas at
once. We will show you what the published evidence says, and we will show
you what thousands of careful people have observed, and we will label
which is which so you can weigh them yourself.

## What we are not

We are not doctors, and nothing here is medical advice. We do not endorse
any substance, brand, or protocol, and we have no financial stake in what
you buy. We work hard to keep bots and undisclosed marketing out, because
a community like this only works if you can trust that the person on the
other end is real.

## The ask

Be specific. Be honest about what did not work. Share your sources. Treat
the people here the way you wish the last forum had treated you.
