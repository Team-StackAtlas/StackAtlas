# About StackAtlas

## Why this exists

Most of us ended up here the same way. You had a question about something you were taking, or thinking about taking, and the places you looked gave you nothing useful. A forum thread where the top answer was "it's good." A review that read like the brand wrote it. Five people giving five different doses and five different times to take it, and nobody agreeing on whether it did anything at all. And somewhere in the back of your mind, the question of whether the person you were reading was even a person.

StackAtlas is an attempt to do better than that, together.

## What this is

A community of people sharing what they actually did and what actually happened, in enough detail to be useful to the next person. Share as much of your health journey as you are comfortable sharing: the dose, the timing, the brand, what changed, what did not, and what you would do differently. The specifics are the point. "It worked for me" helps no one. "Three weeks at 400 mg with dinner, sleep improved, stomach did not" helps everyone.

Alongside the community sits the evidence. Every substance in the Atlas carries the studies behind it, linked to the source, checked against the original record, and labeled honestly for how strong it is. We are big on high-quality sources. We would rather show you one good trial than a page of citations nobody checked.

## What we believe

Mainstream consensus is usually right, and it is where you should start. It is also sometimes behind. Not because anyone is hiding anything, but because research takes money and time, and a lot of what people in this space care about has not had enough of either yet. We hold both ideas at once. We show you what the published evidence says, we show you what careful people have observed, and we label which is which so you can weigh them yourself.

## What this is not

We are not doctors, and nothing here is medical advice. We do not endorse any substance, brand, or protocol, and we have no financial stake in what you buy. We work hard to keep bots and undisclosed marketing out, because a community like this only works if you can trust that the person on the other end is real.

## The ask

Be specific. Be honest about what did not work. Share your sources. Treat the people here the way you wish the last forum had treated you.
