# Refund Policy

**Status: placeholder — StackAtlas currently has no paid products or subscriptions.**

There is nothing to refund today. This page exists so that a refund policy is in place **before** the first paid feature launches, not after.

## Reminder checklist for when paid features arrive

Before enabling any payment flow, replace this page with a real policy that covers:

- [ ] What is sold (subscription, one-time purchase, both) and the billing cycle
- [ ] Refund window and conditions (e.g., full refund within 14 days of first purchase; no refund for partial periods)
- [ ] How to request a refund (in-app button and/or contact email) and the response time
- [ ] Cancellation: how, when it takes effect, and whether access continues to period end
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific rights that cannot be waived (EU/UK 14-day withdrawal right for digital services, Australian Consumer Law, etc.)
- [ ] Chargeback and dispute handling
- [ ] Payment processor named as a data processor in the Privacy Policy (Section 4), and the Terms updated for billing
- [ ] Taxes/VAT handling and invoices
