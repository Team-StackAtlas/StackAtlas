# Cookie & Local Storage Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-20**

## We do not use tracking cookies

StackAtlas sets **no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies**, and loads **no third-party scripts** that would set them. There is nothing here to "accept" in the sense that most cookie banners mean; the notice you see on first visit exists so that you know exactly what is stored and why.

## What is stored in your browser

Everything below is stored in your browser's local storage (not cookies), stays on your device, and is never read by anyone but the StackAtlas app running in your browser. All of it is **strictly necessary** for the feature it serves.

| Key | Purpose | Sent to our servers? |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase auth session | Keeps you signed in. Created only when you sign in. | The session token is sent to our authentication provider to prove it is you. |
| `theme` | Light/dark mode choice. | No |
| `stackatlas_palette` | Color-vision-friendly palette choice. | No |
| `stackatlas_user_scope` | Your access level (Citizen/Explorer) and goal categories. The access level is **never** sent to our servers; goals sync to your profile only if you are signed in. | Goals only, if signed in |
| `stackatlas_consent_notice` | Remembers that you dismissed the first-visit notice. | No |
| `stackatlas_age_confirmed` | Remembers that you confirmed you are 18 or older. | No |
| `stackatlas_units` | Whether you prefer imperial or metric units for displayed body stats. | Only as part of your profile settings, if you save it there |
| `stackatlas_square_tab`, `stackatlas_posts` | Remembers which feed tab you were on and caches posts you already loaded so the page opens faster. | No |
| `user_logs`, `user_notes` | Draft intake logs and notes you have not published. | No, unless you publish them |

## Clearing it

Clearing your browser's site data for StackAtlas removes everything above and signs you out. Nothing about you remains on your device afterward.

## If this changes

If we ever add a cookie or third-party script, this page will list it before it goes live, and the first-visit notice will ask for consent where the law requires it.
